# Solaris: a new architecture for life

**Your health. Your agency. A future we can build together.**

This is our founding direction and the design commitments behind Solaris. The [current-state register](CURRENT-STATE.md) describes what has been inspected, what remains experimental and what still needs to be proved.

## Begin with the whole person

A life does not begin and end at the clinic door. It unfolds through the food we eat, the water we drink, the work that sustains us, the people who care for us and the places we call home. Health belongs in that whole picture.

Yet navigating care can mean telling the same story again, finding records held somewhere else, and adapting to systems that each recognize only one part of us. As more of life moves into software, another question follows: who gets to hold the continuity of that life?

Solaris begins with the conviction that it should remain with the person.

We are building a holistic health ecosystem that connects people, practitioners and communities through care, regenerative agriculture, personal intelligence and shared economic opportunity. Our ambition is to bring the systems that sustain health into a more coherent relationship, while preserving each person's freedom to choose.

## What we mean by a new architecture for life

Architecture determines what a system makes possible. It determines who can give permission, where information lives, what happens when a service disappears and who benefits when value is created.

For Solaris, a new architecture for life means starting with those questions. The person comes first. Care relationships, software, intelligence and payment services must be designed around that person's agency and continuity.

We want to replace the experience of having to surrender control in exchange for convenience: health history tied to an account, personal context trapped in a provider, or care discovery shaped by incentives a person cannot see. We want choices that are understandable, relationships that are accountable and records that people can carry forward.

This work begins in healthcare and is intended to complement practitioners, clinics and public care systems. It builds on open protocols and useful existing tools. The foundations we are changing are the assumptions about ownership, permission, dependence and value.

## Identity first. Local first. The person remains at the center.

**Identity first** means a person's place in the ecosystem must endure beyond any particular login, wallet, device or AI provider. Credentials and keys help establish authority, but they do not define the whole person. They must be replaceable and recoverable through carefully designed processes. A public identifier does not grant access to a medical record or establish a practitioner's qualifications.

**Local first** means designing core health information to remain usable under the person's control, with practical access, export and recovery paths. Connected services can extend what people do. The continuity of their retained records must have an independent path when a service becomes unavailable.

Our engineering principle is explicit:

> No external protocol, company, relay, signer, wallet, AI model, storage provider or interoperability partner may become necessary for the user to retain access to their identity and core health data.

That includes Solaris. It is a requirement against which the software must be tested. Databases, servers and providers still have useful roles; each needs a defined boundary and an exit path. Backups, recovery materials and compatible tools remain essential. Clinical records also have their own custodians and responsibilities: a person's copy and a clinic's record are distinct.

**The person stays at the center. The technology around them can change.**

## Open source as a company commitment

**Solaris is an open-source company by conviction. We are committed to releasing all Solaris-developed software under open-source licenses.** This repository is licensed under [MIT](../LICENSE); each software release must make its own license and source available.

We believe the software people depend on for their health should be open to inspection, improvement and shared progress. A useful discovery should help the next builder begin further ahead. A community should be able to adapt its tools. A person should have a meaningful path to leave a provider and continue with another.

We intend to earn trust through the quality of our work, useful services and accountable relationships. Shared foundations let builders compete on how well they serve people while contributing to progress everyone can build upon.

Open software and private health information belong together. Publishing the tools does not mean publishing the lives entrusted to them.

## Intelligence that works within your permission

Personal intelligence should help a person understand their own life and act with greater agency. Its usefulness should grow from context they choose to provide, with permission that is clear, limited and revocable for future access.

LUCA is the intelligence layer attached to a person's identity within Solaris. We are designing it to work with permitted context and help organize information, explain options, prepare questions and coordinate authorized tasks. Clinical judgment remains with qualified professionals; consequential actions require the appropriate human authority.

Personalization means support that reflects a person's circumstances, goals and preferences. People should be able to inspect and correct what is retained, choose which tools may use it and carry their context to a different provider. Revoking future access cannot erase a copy that has already been delivered, so the sharing boundary matters from the beginning.

Our longer-term ambition is for Solaris to become an open health layer for the agentic web: a way for people's chosen digital assistants to discover practitioners, understand service information and help coordinate care within explicit permissions. Connecting an agent must never, by itself, unlock someone's health records or confer clinical authority.

## Value should return to what makes care possible

Care depends on more than the final transaction. It depends on practitioners, maintainers, infrastructure operators, educators, growers and communities. We want the economic model to recognize the contributions that sustain that work.

**GPS — Global Prosperous Split — is the contribution-sharing model we are developing.** Its purpose is to connect evidence of eligible contribution with transparent allocation rules, accountable review and a record of what was actually paid. Our aim is for value to support the people, open infrastructure and regenerative work that make the ecosystem useful.

The intended model allocates **10% of the full customer payment** on qualifying Solaris ecosystem transactions to a contribution pool. The initial default gives Solaris a larger share of that pool, distributed across separate purpose accounts to support the ecosystem's development. The longer-term direction allows eligible, registered and verified providers to configure the applicable splits for the services or infrastructure they contribute.

The percentage and initial direction are founder-defined targets. The economic contract must specify the exact weights, provider verification, authority to change a split, fees and refund rules before this becomes a live payment promise.

Bitcoin is our chosen monetary foundation for this direction. We want value to move through open monetary infrastructure, with transparent rules and meaningful choice of tools and providers. We are exploring Lightning for Bitcoin payments and RGB for defined, verifiable rights and contract state. GPS determines contribution policy; an authorized payment integration moves funds; verification establishes what happened.

This work is in planning and sandbox exploration. The repository currently describes simulated GPS records and disabled payment routes; a working production allocation system is not established. The [Economic Passport architecture](ECONOMIC-PASSPORT-ARCHITECTURE.md) records the candidate technologies, relationship descriptions and acceptance work.

Our aim is to reward useful contribution throughout the ecosystem without making personal health, public disclosure or access to core records depend on a financial score. Care discovery should make commercial incentives and potential conflicts understandable. The success of a referral must not be mistaken for evidence that a service is right for a person.

## Health connects us to the living world

The clinic, the farm, the water source and the energy that supports daily life belong in the same long-term picture. The wellbeing of people depends on the conditions in which they live.

Solaris's regenerative ambition is to connect care with work that sustains those conditions: nourishing food, healthier land, responsible infrastructure and resilient local communities. New products and services should join because they serve that purpose. Claims about environmental benefit or health improvement need their own evidence, methods and accountable review.

Freedom in healthcare means the ability to ask questions, understand evidence, choose qualified support and participate in decisions about one's own care. We stand for that freedom alongside scientific rigor, honest uncertainty and respect for the living world.

## Build the foundation, then earn the larger promise

Our starting work is concrete: personal health records, practitioner discovery, clinic workflows, permissioned intelligence and private contribution receipts. Each wider promise has to grow from something people can use, inspect and recover.

We want an ecosystem in which better tools strengthen human agency, useful contributions support shared prosperity, and progress in technology serves the health of people and the places that sustain them.

**A future where your health story stays with you, your choices remain yours, and the value we create helps sustain the people and living systems we depend on.**
